LILVDVM is a Moroccan filmmaker and editor working at the edge of reality and fiction. He does not simply capture images; he catches moods, fragments, and stories that need no explanation. His editing is deliberate, atmospheric, and unpredictable, bringing ordinary moments into a different light. Not much is known about the man behind the work; his films speak for themselves. Each frame has a purpose, each cut raises a question, and each project feels like a glimpse into an unknown universe.
