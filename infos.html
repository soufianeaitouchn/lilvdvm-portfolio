01 / 04Who I am

My name is Adam Boulghal, a Moroccan filmmaker, editor, and cinema student based in Ouarzazate. I’m also a member of the Avision crew, a visual production company.

02 / 04How it started

I started working in filmmaking around three years ago, exploring different sides of visual creation, from editing and VFX to 3D and filmmaking.

03 / 04What I’ve made

Since then, I’ve worked on more than 100 projects, including commercials, music videos, promotional content, short films, and other visual projects.

04 / 04Why I do it

I love experimenting, trying new techniques, and turning ideas into something people can actually see and experience.
